History

Vintage Stitches is a solo run clothing and accessory business. It has been operational since 2024 and has grown steadily in it's local community.
Vintage Stitches uses crualty free fabrics for it's products, and prides itself on sustainable business practices.
Although the current rage of products is small, they have always been high quality and enjoyed by the community.
Vintage Stitches prides itself on customer service and puts in extra effort in ensuring each customer is satisfied with their purpose.

Goals

Vintage Stitches is looking to expand at the moment. We are looking to hire new employees and to expand our product range.
We are committed to our values as a business, and will continue to employ sustainable business practices throughout our growth.
