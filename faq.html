Frequently asked questions about orders, sizes and handmade knitwear

Find answers about product availability, pickup, sizing and how to contact us about custom or business enquiries.

  • Q. When can I pickup?
    A. You will receive an email/phone call when your order is ready to pickup.

  • Q. How long will my order take?
    A. This depending on stock, your may have to wait for productions to be made.

  • Q. Can I change the size of my item?
    A. Due to the small nature of production, there is only one size of item at the moments.

  • Q. How do I know when products on offer change?
    A. The catalogue will be updated accordingly. New editions will be presented on the homepage.

  • Q. I'm interested in working with you, how do I get in touch?
    A. You may contact me via email. See about us for more info.
